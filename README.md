# E-Commerce Business Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce business performance using **Microsoft Power BI**.

An interactive dashboard was developed to provide a centralized view of important business metrics related to sales, revenue, orders, marketing activities, customer behavior, and operational performance.

The purpose of the project is to convert raw business data into meaningful visual insights that can help management understand performance, identify trends, compare different business segments, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large amounts of data from sales transactions, customers, marketing activities, website visits, payments, deliveries, and returns.

Analyzing these areas separately can make it difficult for management to understand the overall business performance.

The business needs an interactive reporting solution that can help answer questions such as:

- How is the business performing overall?
- Which regions and product categories generate the most revenue?
- How effective are marketing activities?
- Which customer segments contribute the most to sales?
- Are delivery and return operations performing well?
- How does customer satisfaction vary across products and regions?

This project addresses these requirements through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall e-commerce sales and revenue performance
- Monitor order volume and website activity
- Compare performance across regions and product categories
- Analyze marketing spend and its relationship with revenue
- Understand customer segments and repeat customer behavior
- Analyze payment preferences
- Evaluate delivery performance
- Monitor return rates and shipping costs
- Analyze customer satisfaction
- Provide insights to support data-driven business decisions

# 📑 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is organized into three pages, with each page focusing on a specific business area.

## 1. Executive Overview

The Executive Overview provides a high-level summary of the business performance.

### Key Performance Indicators

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Website Visits
- Total Marketing Spend
- Average Customer Rating
- Average Delivery Days

### Visual Analysis

- Revenue by Region
- Orders by Product Category
- Revenue Trend Over Time
- Payment Mode Distribution

### Interactive Filters

- Region
- Product Category
- Customer Segment
- Repeat Customer

This page allows users to quickly understand the overall business performance and identify major trends.

---

## 2. Sales & Marketing Performance

This page focuses on analyzing sales performance and marketing activities.

### Visual Analysis

- Revenue by Product Category
- Marketing Spend by Region
- Website Visits vs Orders
- Marketing Spend vs Revenue
- Revenue by Customer Segment

### Interactive Filters

- Region
- Product Category
- Customer Segment

This page helps evaluate how marketing activities and website traffic relate to business performance.

## 3. Customer & Operational Insights

This page focuses on customer satisfaction and operational performance.

### Visual Analysis

### Interactive Filters

- Customer Segment
- Product Category

This page helps identify customer satisfaction patterns and operational areas that may require improvement.
